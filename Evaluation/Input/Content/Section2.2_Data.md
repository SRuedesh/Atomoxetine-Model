### 2.2.2 Clinical data

The source article reports that the Atomoxetine PBPK model was developed and evaluated with oral single-dose and multiple-dose plasma concentration-time profiles. The clinical studies represented in this evaluation include Belle 2002, Cui 2007, Nakano 2016, Byeon 2015, Kim 2018, Sauer 2003, and Todor 2016.

The following publications were used for model building:

| Publication | Arm / Treatment / Information used for model building |
| :--- | :--- |
| [Nakano 2016](#5-references) | Plasma PK profiles after single oral administration of 50 mg Atomoxetine as solution or capsule in genotyped extensive metabolizers |
| [Byeon 2015](#5-references) | Plasma PK profile after single oral administration of 40 mg Atomoxetine in CYP2D6 AS 2 subjects |
| [Kim 2018](#5-references) | Plasma PK profile after single oral administration of 20 mg Atomoxetine in CYP2D6 AS 2 subjects |
| [Sauer 2003](#5-references) | Plasma PK profiles after repeated oral administration of 20 mg Atomoxetine in genotyped extensive and poor metabolizers |

The following dosing scenarios were simulated and compared to respective data for model verification:

| Scenario | Data reference |
| :--- | :--- |
| Once daily oral administration of 20 mg Atomoxetine in genotyped extensive metabolizers | [Belle 2002](#5-references) |
| Single oral administration of 40 mg Atomoxetine and once daily oral administration of 80 mg Atomoxetine in CYP2D6 AS 1 subjects | [Cui 2007](#5-references) |
| Single oral administration of 40 mg Atomoxetine in CYP2D6 AS 0.5 and AS 1.25 subjects | [Byeon 2015](#5-references) |
| Single oral administration of 20 mg Atomoxetine in CYP2D6 AS 0.5 subjects | [Kim 2018](#5-references) |
| Single oral administration of 25 mg Atomoxetine in genotyped normal and poor metabolizers | [Todor 2016](#5-references) |
