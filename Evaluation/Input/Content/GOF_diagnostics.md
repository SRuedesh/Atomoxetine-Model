Goodness-of-fit diagnostics summarize the agreement between predicted and observed Atomoxetine plasma concentrations for model-building and verification simulations.

The source article reports Atomoxetine mean GMFE values of 1.20 for AUClast and 1.18 for Cmax in the base-model evaluation. The supplement reports DGI ratio GMFE values of 1.25 for AUClast ratios and 1.28 for Cmax ratios.
