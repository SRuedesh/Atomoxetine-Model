The PBPK model for Atomoxetine was developed and evaluated with clinical pharmacokinetic data after oral administration.

The model was built and evaluated covering data from studies including in particular:

* single-dose and repeated-dose oral administration,
* oral solution, capsule, and tablet formulations,
* CYP2D6 extensive, normal, intermediate, and poor metabolizer settings represented by genotype, phenotype, or activity score.

The model quantifies CYP2D6-mediated metabolism, CYP2C19-mediated non-CYP2D6 metabolism, and passive glomerular filtration.

The source article reported good Atomoxetine base-model performance. Across Atomoxetine base-model evaluations, the reported mean GMFE was 1.20 for AUClast and 1.18 for Cmax. For plasma concentration values, 88.9% of Atomoxetine predictions were within two-fold of the corresponding observed values.

The source supplement reports Atomoxetine base-model and DGI model evaluation for Cui 2007, Byeon 2015, Kim 2018, Sauer 2003, and Todor 2016. Reported DGI ratio performance was 5/5 AUClast ratios and 5/5 Cmax ratios within the prediction success limits, with GMFE values of 1.25 for AUClast ratios and 1.28 for Cmax ratios.

In this evaluation, the merged GOF diagnostic over the included concentration observations gives a GMFE of 1.27. The concentration-time profiles and GOF diagnostics support the ability of the model to describe Atomoxetine plasma exposure across the represented CYP2D6 activity-score and phenotype groups.
